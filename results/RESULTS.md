## Deviations from the paper

- All experiments use synthetic inputs (model parameters only); no external market data is required by any of the five experiments.
- Experiment 3 (variance swap): the strike truncation for the call integral from S0 to ∞ is set at 5×S0, not specified in the notes.
- Experiment 4 (Dupire): the SVI parameter bounds and initialization are not specified in the notes; conservative defaults (a≥0, b≥0, ρ∈[−1,1], m∈ℝ, ξ≥0) are used.
- Experiment 5 (Heston): the notes intentionally omit the Riccati solution for C and D; the Fourier-cosine method is used as the pricing engine rather than an analytical closed form.
- Experiment 5 calibration uses a synthetic implied-vol surface because no market dataset is provided in the notes.

## Not reproduced

- Empirical implied-volatility surface calibration with real market option quotes (Experiments 3, 4, 5): no dataset is provided in the notes, and loading a live option surface was not required by the experiments as specified.
